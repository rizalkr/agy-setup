# Conversion Copywriting & Anti-AI-Slop

## 1. Anti-AI-Slop Mandate
- Ban generic AI filler and hype clichés: `delve`, `elevate`, `unleash`, `revolutionize`, `seamlessly`, `game-changer`, `in today's fast-paced digital landscape`, `empower`, `next-gen`, `supercharge`, `unlock the power of`, rhetorical "Here's the kicker", and symmetrical 3-adjective lists.
- Ban hollow claims without mechanism or proof: replace "blazingly fast" or "enterprise-grade" with exact latency, benchmarks, limits, or the technical mechanism.
- Default to active voice, concrete nouns, specific numbers, and varied sentence cadence.

## 2. Core Conversion Frameworks
- **PAS (Problem -> Agitation -> Solution)**: Name the exact friction, quantify the cost of inaction or manual workarounds, then present the product mechanism as the direct fix.
- **BAB (Before -> After -> Bridge)**: Contrast the painful status quo with the measurable outcome, then show the workflow bridge.
- **JTBD Headline Formula**: `[Specific Outcome] without [Primary Friction]` or `[Actionable Capability] for [Target Persona] in [Timeframe/Constraint]`.
- **Feature -> Mechanism -> Outcome**: Never list a feature in isolation; state what it does technically (Mechanism) and why the user benefits (Outcome).

## 3. Landing Page & Hero Architecture
- **Eyebrow**: Category anchor or concrete differentiator (3-6 words, zero hype).
- **H1 Headline**: Specific value proposition in plain language (6-12 words, clear within 3 seconds).
- **Subheadline**: Explain *how* it works and *who* it is for (1-2 sentences, max 25 words).
- **Primary CTA**: Action verb + specific value (`Start Free Trial`, `Deploy in 60s`, `Explore Live Demo`) instead of vague `Submit` or `Learn More`, paired with risk-reversal microcopy (`No credit card required`, `Open-source MIT`).

## 4. UX Microcopy & Product Interface
- **Buttons & Actions**: Verb + Noun matching the immediate consequence (`Revoke API Key`, `Export CSV`).
- **Empty States**: State what belongs here, why it matters, and provide one primary action button to populate it.
- **Error Messages**: State what happened, why it occurred, and the exact recovery step; never blame the user or expose raw codes alone.

## 5. Technical Blog, Case Studies & Social Copy
- **Hook (First 2 Lines)**: Lead with a counter-intuitive finding, hard metric (`74% fewer tokens`), or concrete engineering failure mode—never a generic dictionary definition.
- **Structure**: Problem context -> Architecture and trade-offs -> Concrete code/config proof -> Measured outcome.
- **Self-Edit Checklist**: Cut ~20% word count on a second pass; replace every abstract adjective with a number, mechanism, or concrete example.
